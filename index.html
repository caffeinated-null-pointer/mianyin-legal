眠音 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日

「眠音」是一款助眠 App，会根据你的睡眠和身体状态，在手机上实时生成适合入睡的声音。你的数据只在你的 iPhone 上处理。眠音不联网，我们没有服务器，不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据。

1. 健康数据（HealthKit）

在你同意后，眠音会从 Apple「健康」读取：睡眠记录、静息心率、心率变异性、呼吸频率、步数和正念时间。这些数据只用于在手机本地判断你今晚的状态（比如最近是否睡得少、夜里是否容易醒），并据此搭配声音。

眠音只读取，不会往「健康」里写入任何数据。健康数据绝不会用于广告，也不会分享给任何第三方。你可以随时在「设置 → 健康 → 数据访问与设备 → 眠音」里关闭授权。

2. 本地 AI

今晚的声音配方由 iPhone 上的 Apple 智能本地模型生成；不支持 Apple 智能的设备使用手机上的规则计算。整个过程都在手机上完成，数据不发送到任何服务器。

3. 偏好学习

早上你给昨晚打的分、每次播放了哪些声音，会保存在眠音自己的手机存储里，用来学习你喜欢的声音。这些记录只存在你的手机上，在设置里可以一键清除，删除 App 也会一并清除。

4. 耳机动作

如果你开启「头部追踪」，眠音会读取 AirPods 等耳机报告的头部转动方向，让声音停留在原位。这些数据只在播放时实时使用，不会保存。

5. 我们不做的事

  • 不需要注册账号，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话或位置。
  • 没有广告，没有第三方统计或追踪工具。
  • 不使用麦克风，不录音。
  • 不出售或分享任何数据。

6. 儿童

眠音不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

7. 健康提示

眠音不是医疗产品，不能诊断或治疗失眠等疾病。如果长期睡不好，请咨询医生。

Mianyin Privacy Policy

Effective: 30 October 2026

Mianyin is a sleep app that generates sleep sounds on your iPhone based on your sleep and how your body is doing. Your data is processed only on your iPhone. Mianyin does not use the internet, we run no servers, and we collect, upload or sell no personal data.

Health data (HealthKit)

With your permission, Mianyin reads sleep analysis, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, steps and mindful minutes from Apple Health, only to choose tonight's sounds on your device. Mianyin never writes to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared with third parties. You can turn access off at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Mianyin.

On-device AI and learning

Tonight's sound recipe is produced by Apple's on-device Apple Intelligence model, or by on-device rules on devices without it. Your morning ratings and what was played are stored only in the app's own storage on your phone to learn what you like; you can clear them in Settings, and deleting the app removes them.

Headphone motion

If you turn on head tracking, Mianyin reads the head orientation reported by your headphones while playing so sounds stay in place. It is used in real time and never stored.

What we don't do

  • No account, and no name, email, phone number or location collected.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No microphone or recording.
  • No selling or sharing of any data.

Health notice

Mianyin is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat insomnia or any other condition. If you have ongoing sleep problems, please see a doctor.
